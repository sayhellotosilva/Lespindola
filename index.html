I'm a Brazilian-Canadian passionate about audiovisual, music, art and photography!

With a Bachelor's degree in Radio, TV, and Internet and a certificate in Graphic Design for Print and Web, I've been fortunate to work in various creative roles. From TV channels to advertising agencies and startups, I've gained an invaluable experience along the way as Assistant Director, Filmmaker, and Producer.

Passionate and dedicated, I love bringing ideas to life and creating captivating visuals.

Currently, I am an Associate Director and Senior Graphic Operator for live TV in Toronto, and looking to relocate to Spain in January 2027.

Alongside my role, I freelance and volunteer as a Photographer, collaborating with independent businesses, bands and events to create visuals and engaging social media content.
